When to tool: Context-dependent tool use by Goffin’s cockatoos during cavity foraging

Goffin Lab · University of Veterinary Medicine Vienna

Investigated whether tool deployment during cavity foraging helps Goffin’s cockatoos mitigate aversion to narrow, dark spaces. Conducted 10 behavioural test sessions per subject with 11 habituated cockatoos.

Phenotypic reassessment of Yellow-bellied Bulbul subspecies classification

Avian Evolution Lab · National University of Singapore

Quantitatively analysed 78 museum specimens and citizen-science photographs. Measured plumage colour and morphology using ImageJ-based colorimetry and standard biometrics, and evaluated variation using LDA and Amadon’s 75% rule. Also assisted with tropical bird mist-netting and biometric data collection.
